Valley IT | Department of Defense

Senior UX/UI Designer

Due to the sensitive nature of some projects with the Department of Defense (DOD), I am only allowed to show certain pieces

Valley IT is a company that provides software and hardware solutions to various industries with the Department of Defense being one of the major clients. As the Lead and Senior UX designer, I implemented:

  • Material Design conepts
  • Design pattern libraries
  • Worked on Command and Control (C2) products
  • Led cross-functional collaboration with product owners, developers, and stakeholders

    • One of the major focus points that I worked on was C5ISR Platform which is a modular, high‑reliability decision‑support system designed to integrate intelligence, communication, and operational data into a unified interface for crisis‑response and defense teams. My role focused on translating complex technical workflows into intuitive, human‑centered interactions that support rapid situational awareness and coordinated action.

    K-Chat

    K‑Chat is a secure, enterprise‑grade messaging platform that supports multimedia communication, advanced Push‑to‑Talk, classified message handling, real‑time translation in 50 languages, and multi‑threaded chat management. As the sole UX designer, I led a complete end‑to‑end redesign of both the mobile and web applications, rebuilding the experience from the ground up to improve clarity, usability, and operational efficiency. The redesigned system supports near‑real‑time communication, seamless file sharing, and full XMPP compatibility.
